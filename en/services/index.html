How we can help

Specialized, not generic

We’re not a development agency or a generic manual-testing vendor. We work as a consultancy specialized in software quality and Adobe Commerce, bringing an independent technical perspective at every stage: evaluation, migration, ongoing implementation, or continuous platform evolution.

Service lines

What you can engage us for

Services grouped in a clear, commercial way, each focused on a concrete quality or Adobe Commerce problem.

  • Adobe Commerce Advisory

    We support teams evaluating Adobe Commerce, planning a migration, or already running the platform and needing an independent view on scope, risk, and functional decisions. We help distinguish native functionality from customizations or extensions, and prepare the organization for UAT, go-live, and the platform’s ongoing evolution.

    • Platform Fit Assessment
    • Migration Readiness Assessment
    • Implementation Risk Review
    • Adobe Commerce functional analysis
    • Third-party extension and module analysis (incl. the Amasty ecosystem)
    • UAT and acceptance strategy
    • Pre-launch / go-live readiness
    • Business user enablement

    We work as an independent quality and operations advisory, not as platform implementers.

  • QA Consulting & Test Strategy

    We help define or improve how a team assures the quality of its products: what to test, how deeply, and in what order. This includes risk-based prioritization, scope definition, regression strategy, acceptance and quality criteria, and release preparation, always adapted to the team’s real context and constraints.

    • Test strategy
    • Risk-based analysis and prioritization
    • Test scope definition
    • Regression strategy
    • Acceptance and quality criteria
    • Release readiness
    • QA process optimization
    • Technical team enablement

  • QA Automation

    We design and evolve maintainable automation suites for web and e-commerce applications, focused on end-to-end regression. We use Playwright and Python under a Page Object Model architecture, fixtures, and clear reporting, leaving the automation ready for future integration into CI/CD pipelines.

    • Playwright
    • Python
    • Page Object Model
    • Fixtures & test data
    • E2E automation
    • Automated regression
    • Reporting
    • CI/CD integration readiness

  • Adobe Commerce QA

    Functional and regression testing specialized in the Adobe Commerce / Magento ecosystem: storefront, Admin, catalog, categories, promotions, checkout when applicable, modules and extensions, integrations, patches, and upgrades. We validate that customizations and platform changes don’t introduce regressions.

    • Storefront and Admin
    • Catalog, categories, and promotions
    • Modules and extensions
    • Integrations
    • Patches and upgrades
    • Checkout (when applicable)
    • Post-platform-change validation

  • Web & E-commerce QA

    Functional, regression, integration, and end-to-end testing for web and e-commerce experiences in general, including release validation and pre- and post-deployment validation. We apply the same discipline used on Adobe Commerce to other enterprise web applications when the technical fit justifies it.

    • Functional testing
    • Regression testing
    • Integration testing
    • End-to-end testing
    • Release validation
    • Pre-production validation
    • Post-deployment validation

  • Technical SEO & Analytics QA

    We verify that technical SEO implementations meet expectations (metadata, canonical, robots.txt, X-Robots-Tag, sitemap, ALT, Open Graph, URLs, and missing resources) and that digital measurement is correctly configured (GA4, GTM, dataLayer, events, parameters, and triggers), validating consistency between functional behavior and tracking. This is not SEO strategy or marketing consulting.

    • Metadata, canonical, and robots.txt
    • Sitemap and X-Robots-Tag
    • ALT and Open Graph
    • URL structure
    • GA4 and GTM
    • DataLayer, events, and triggers
    • Functional vs. tracking consistency

  • Quality Engineering & Process Improvement

    We work on the structure and maturity of the quality practice: standardization, test architecture, repository organization, documentation, and QA workflows. We support the transition from manual processes to automation and the adoption of AI-assisted Quality Engineering practices, along with team training and enablement.

    • Standardization and best practices
    • Test architecture
    • Repository organization
    • QA documentation and workflows
    • Manual-to-automation transition
    • AI-assisted Quality Engineering
    • Team training and enablement
