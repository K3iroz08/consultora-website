Applied Experience

  • Financial Services

    Regression Automation for Financial Services

    Challenge
    Validating environments required manually reviewing a considerable volume of pages before each release, creating repetitive effort and making it difficult to systematically identify differences.
    Intervention
    Design of an automated regression strategy to crawl target pages, compare environments, and generate evidence of structural, visual, and content differences.
    Impact
    Turned a largely manual activity into a repeatable, traceable, and scalable process, freeing the team to focus on analyzing the differences that matter.

    Quality Strategy for E-commerce on Adobe Commerce

    Challenge
    A retail platform with multiple journeys needed continuous validation of catalog, navigation, customer accounts, promotions, integrations, and transactional processes.
    Intervention
    Design and execution of QA strategies on Adobe Commerce, combining functional testing, regression, integration validation, and coverage of critical business flows, including PDP, PLP, user accounts, promotions, checkout, and integrations.
    Impact
    Structured quality coverage built around the processes that sustain e-commerce operations, prioritizing functional and business risk.

    Technical QA and SEO at Scale

    Challenge
    On sites with hundreds of URLs, manually checking metadata, canonicals, indexation, Open Graph, images, and other technical elements makes it difficult to maintain consistent coverage.
